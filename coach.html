AI career coach

Ask FinCoach anything about finance careers.

Track guidance, role explainers, study plans, and mock interviews — answered from this platform's own guides, track roadmaps, role profiles, and desk dictionary. The model runs entirely on your device: nothing you type is uploaded or stored anywhere.

Open-source engine

Run the model on your device

FinCoach uses WebLLM — an open-source, in-browser inference engine — with an open-weights model. The first load downloads the model (one time, then cached).

Grounded answers

How it answers

Every question is matched against 129 chunks of this platform's content — the 4 track roadmaps, 40 learning modules, 16 role profiles, desk-lab guides, and dictionary — and the model answers from those sources, with links.

No WebGPU on this device? FinCoach falls back to guide search: the same retrieval, answered as source cards instead of generated text.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your browser

Same promise as the resume analyzer: no account, no upload, no tracking. Close the tab and the conversation is gone. The model files come from public open-source mirrors; your questions never do.

FinCoach is a study aid, not a career counselor. It can be wrong — verify anything consequential (salaries, hiring timelines, credentials) against primary sources. Interview feedback reflects the platform's guides, not real hiring decisions.
